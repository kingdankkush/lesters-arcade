Support

Email kingdankkush420@gmail.com for gameplay problems, privacy questions or accessibility barriers.

For a bug report, include the game, browser/device, what you expected, what happened and steps to reproduce it. A cropped screenshot or a voluntarily exported diagnostic report can help. Review reports before sharing and remove information you do not want to send.

Never send a seed phrase, private key, password or authentication code. A public transaction hash may help investigate a testnet problem; do not send funds to obtain support. No response-time guarantee is offered.

Privacy

The arcade uses browser storage for local profiles, settings, progression, run history and cached game resources. Some wallet-related views also cache public wallet addresses or transaction details. Local storage is not a verified on-chain record.

You can clear this site's data through your browser settings. Doing so can remove local progress and preferences. It does not delete public blockchain records, copies you exported or messages already sent to support.

The portal loads Vercel Web Analytics on its production and Vercel-hosted domains. Hosting and analytics services process request and usage information. Wallet and RPC providers process requests when their features are used; public wallet addresses and blockchain transactions can be publicly visible. Do not assume testnet activity is private.

If you contact support, your email provider and the operator receive the information you choose to send so the request can be handled. Send only what is necessary. For access or deletion questions about information held by the operator, use the support address above. Technical and legal limits can apply, particularly to public blockchain records.

Terms of use

Use the arcade lawfully and without disrupting other players, abusing the service or attempting unauthorized access. Do not present altered, simulated or local-only results as verified competition results. Report vulnerabilities privately to support.

The arcade is experimental. Features, local saves, testnet integrations and availability may change or fail. Keep any records you need, and do not rely on game scores or token displays for financial decisions.

Access to the site does not transfer ownership of its software, artwork or other content. Existing licenses and applicable rights remain in effect. Nothing in this notice is intended to remove rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Accessibility

This information page works without game JavaScript and provides keyboard-accessible links and headings. The games use canvas graphics and real-time visual action, which may present barriers. Available input and display settings vary by game and device; consult the in-game settings and help.

No accessibility-conformance certification is claimed. Physical-device and assistive-technology coverage is not complete. If a feature blocks you, contact support with the page/game, device, assistive technology if relevant, and the task you were trying to complete. An accessible alternative can be discussed without promising a specific fix date.

Testnet and rewards notice

Do not send real assets or use valuable wallets to test experimental features. LitVM LiteForge is a testnet integration. Test tokens, scores, previews and simulated settlement are not promises of money, prizes or future rewards.

Live settlement is disabled in this candidate: SETTLEMENT_LIVE=false. Local Ranked previews and locally cached sessions are not proof of verified settlement. A connected wallet alone does not make a result official.

Only approve a wallet request after checking its network and details. Wallet signatures and blockchain transactions may have consequences that the website cannot reverse. No mainnet launch or real-funds payout is announced by this notice.
