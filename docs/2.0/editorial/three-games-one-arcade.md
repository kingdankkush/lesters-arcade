## Start with a free run

Some nights call for a last stand. Others call for a clean jump or the satisfaction of a row falling into place. Lester’s Arcade puts three different games within reach of the same browser.

**Free Mode is the place to start.** Choose a cabinet, get a feel for its controls and find the game that makes you want another attempt. You do not need a wallet to practise.

## Hard Money Heroes

The Crypto Wasteland is a place to keep moving. Hard Money Heroes is a top-down survival shooter: choose a hero, fight the horde and work towards field objectives while building your run.

- Move with WASD or the arrow keys on a keyboard.
- Aim with the mouse, or use the separate movement and aim controls on a phone.
- Leave yourself room to move. A clear route matters as much as the next target.

Your first run is a chance to learn the space and the weapons. On the next one, try a different build and see what changes.

## Chikun’s Escape

Chikun’s Escape trades the last stand for forward motion. Run, jump and fly through Ground & Sky, picking up coins and finding a path through the hazards.

Tap, click or press Space to jump and flap. Release to descend and land; running resumes on the ground. The rhythm comes from judging when to rise and when to let Chikun drop.

> A clean landing can be more useful than one more flap.

## STACKED

STACKED is the cabinet for falling blocks, clean rows and the arcade soundtrack. Move, rotate, hold and drop your pieces while keeping enough space for the next arrival.

Review the control guide before your first run. Give yourself a few pieces to settle in, and watch where each placement leaves the board. A tidy stack gives you more options when the shape you want does not arrive.

## Pick your next cabinet

Switch games when you want a different pace. There is no need to make your first choice your only one.
