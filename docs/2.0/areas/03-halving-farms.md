# Halving Farms — area brief draft

Status: local Farms W2c geometry/nav/native-browser checkpoint passes; owner playtest, full area design and art acceptance remain open. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** Fields, yards and access tracks explain how a working farm was used. Crop rows organise sightlines; machinery and farm buildings stay at practical places rather than scattered across the playable floor.

**Landmark.** A windmill above the main barn yard, with silos as a secondary silhouette.

**Routes in and out.** Farm road to MWEB Meadows and an outer track into Rugpull Woods. A yard loop and a field-side route join both entrances without crossing through a sealed objective.

**Objective staging.** Put the barn-door objective on the actual barn, with a rutted access track ending at its threshold. Separate the interaction apron from enemy approach lanes and ensure the closed door does not block the only road through the area.

**Combat and height.** Alternate open field fights with sheltered yard courts. Gentle banks provide height options; solid barn walls and low stacked timber provide cover. A broad yard offers two exits and room to dodge without tiny crop props becoming invisible collision.

**Secret.** A field-edge track and a visibly imperfect hedge lead to a small storage nook, then back to the farm loop. Keep reward details versioned and unbalanced until the measured gameplay slice.

**Art sub-palette.** Olive #85846A, weathered timber #665044, soil #625A47; board L11/L12.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Rows and ruts guide travel while avoiding high-frequency stripe noise. Barn surfaces use quiet weathered material rather than cue-like glowing red. Check field routes with the actual nav grid and ordinary human-scale actors.
