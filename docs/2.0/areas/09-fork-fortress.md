# Fork Fortress — area brief draft

Status: preparatory layout intent; no geometry/nav/playtest/art acceptance yet. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** A fortified enemy compound occupies high ground, with gates, ramparts, work yards and a keep that explain its hierarchy. Machinery supports a human/zombie enemy organisation.

**Landmark.** The keep and gatehouse silhouette above the approach.

**Routes in and out.** High trail from Ledger Ridge and a long winding lower service approach toward MWEB Meadows. Its danger must be visible, and measured shortest paths/threats must support difficulty with distance before approval. A courtyard loop joins both entrances; an objective gate cannot strand the only connection.

**Objective staging.** Place gate controls at practical posts with readable approaches and sightlines to the gate. Put the 51% Foreman encounter in the keep/work court, with quarry equipment at the perimeter and human-scale access. The gatehouse-to-keep threshold creates anticipation; review entrances, exits and pre-fight/fight/after-fight gate states explicitly rather than assuming access rules are solved by the art.

**Combat and height.** Prototype a 1,800–2,400-unit court with clear entrance threshold, two legal exits/return routes, peripheral tall/short cover and rampart elevation. Walls/towers/flush decks need exact source-to-collider alignment; every climb/drop remains a gated traversal contract.

**Secret.** A service route through storage or a broken rampart access leads to a reachable nook and returns to the courtyard. Avoid decorative doors that imply interaction the greybox cannot provide.

**Art sub-palette.** Iron #424B4B, masonry #7B7C70, worn cloth #635F68; board L22/L23/L24.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** The Foreman remains a human or zombie with equipment, never a mech proxy. Keep machinery emission subdued and bosses/tells readable. Check multi-level directed reachability, gate/full-rebuild parity and exits before area art.
