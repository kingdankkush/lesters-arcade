# Scrypt Bayou — area brief draft

Status: preparatory layout intent; no geometry/nav/playtest/art acceptance yet. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** A channel-management settlement built around water, cypress roots and raised timber routes. The lock, winches and walkways form one believable system.

**Landmark.** The lock gate and wheel tower at a narrow channel crossing.

**Routes in and out.** Boardwalk/coastal approach from Silver Coast and a riverside path to Hashwood River. Both approaches reach a dry staging platform; closing the lock must not remove the only legal connection.

**Objective staging.** Put the Lock interaction on the channel control structure, within a clear apron and visible mechanical line to the gate. Keep the Lockkeeper arena beside this system rather than in unrelated open marsh. A readable bridge/gate threshold gives an anticipation space before entry; review arena entrances, exits and gate states explicitly in the versioned encounter slice.

**Combat and height.** Prototype a 1,800–2,400-unit dry arena around the gate machinery, with quiet floor, two readable ways out, balanced peripheral cover heights and a raised maintenance deck. Narrow boardwalk approaches target 300–600 units and must remain navigable under crowd load.

**Secret.** A marked side plank route leads to a stilt-store nook and returns to the main walkway. Its edges stay visible through atmosphere; decorative reeds cannot disguise legal path width.

**Art sub-palette.** Olive #586451, wet timber #494D3F, water #344C48; board L10/L14/L15.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Deep water is blocked and visibly distinct. Wet walkable ground uses existing movement in the first greybox; a slowdown is a separate unapproved rules choice. Fog sits behind readable actors/tells. Check boardwalk edges, gate parity and ordinary human scale.
