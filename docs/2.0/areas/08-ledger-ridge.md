# Ledger Ridge — area brief draft

Status: local Ridge W2f geometry/nav/native-browser checkpoint passes; owner playtest, full area design and art acceptance remain open. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** A quarry road climbs through layered rock toward the fortress. Switchbacks and narrow crossings make the mountain legible and the approach deliberate.

**Landmark.** A quarry cut and suspended rope crossing beneath the fortress skyline.

**Routes in and out.** Quarry road from Litecoin City and high trail to Fork Fortress. The traversal prototype may offer an alternate ledge route, but the baseline connected greybox must not depend on an unimplemented climb.

**Objective staging.** Place the proposed uplink on a serviceable overlook with line of sight, an access track and an obvious interaction face. Machinery and cables describe its function without becoming new active hazards.

**Combat and height.** Use 300–600-unit switchback passages interrupted by wider landings with two exits. Solid rock faces and lower quarry barriers provide two cover heights; a clear shelf creates an elevation option. Landings for climbs/drops must be fully specified and independently tested before activation.

**Secret.** A side quarry track reaches a visibly different rock shelf and rejoins the switchback. Measure ledge directionality and return paths; the secret must not depend on a border-nav halo bug.

**Art sub-palette.** Stone #7E817A, cool shadow #515E66, sparse pine #58685D; board L19/L20.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Height, path width, cliff faces and rope-bridge decking take priority over decorative gravel. Oracle Marksman placements are versioned gameplay work; do not use a visual prototype to add sniper AI or change damage.

## W2f local layout checkpoint

Ledger Ridge uses a quarry road bent around rock fingers and a lower working
landing. The fixed City entrance reaches both routes: the main track turns west,
north and east across a shallow maintenance shelf before joining the Fortress
trail; the lower service route passes through the open southeast landing. A
blocked quarry cut must not remove that lower connection. Neither route requires
climbing, dropping or a new movement permission.

The western rock face and inner quarry cuts physically frame the road. Their
oblique corners replace generic corner buildings. The clear inspection junction
sits between close north/south cut faces, giving nearby landmarks in the phone
walking view. The upper maintenance shelf has a ramp at each end, and its uplink
marker faces the headframe equipment against the rock. A branch track reaches a
store tucked northwest of the quarry and rejoins the upper road. The southeast
working landing keeps two clear exits and sparse edge cover in two heights.

Future terrain and assets follow excavation and service use:

- Rock cuts show layered strata, tool scars and rubble at stable feet and cut
  ends. Keep the turning corridors legible; do not spread boulders uniformly.
- The quarry road uses compacted gravel with wheel wear through broad turns,
  drainage at the rock side and a readable outside edge. The lower service route
  looks more worn near its working landing and store access.
- The headframe, cables and control face belong together. Place maintenance
  materials behind or beside the equipment, leaving the access shelf clear.
- The store has a loading face toward its branch track; stock belongs along the
  back and side walls. The secret marker names a reachable nook, not a new reward.
- The open landing keeps its centre quiet for movement and tells. Barriers and
  wall fragments sit at working edges; avoid filling empty space with repeated
  crates merely for density.
- Sparse pines belong on stable shelves away from traffic and equipment. A rope
  crossing, deep drop or climbable face still needs its separate approved slice.

This local slice uses only current solid/ground/ramp geometry in the isolated local
preview. The shelf is a small 24-unit navigation option, not final mountain-scale
elevation. Production art, actor combat, sniper AI, traversal controls, owner
playtest and official map/version acceptance remain open. Local source and
browser checks pass; flat greybox art, sparse yard space and tiny/overlapping
labels remain for later work.
