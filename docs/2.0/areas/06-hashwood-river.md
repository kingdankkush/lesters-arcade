# Hashwood River — area brief draft

Status: preparatory layout intent; no geometry/nav/playtest/art acceptance yet. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** The river explains the bridges and settlement tracks. Forest routes alternate between enclosed banks and a broad clearing occupied by a travelling spectacle.

**Landmark.** A waterfall shelf and bridge silhouette; the Baron’s marquee is the secondary local landmark.

**Routes in and out.** City road, Bayou riverside trail, Rugpull Woods path and a darker Hollow Pines trail. Place crossings at narrow banks and provide more than one route across the area; do not force every run through the boss clearing.

**Objective staging.** Stage river equipment at a bridge or actual channel structure. The Rug Pull Baron entrance crosses a visible threshold into the marquee clearing, with anticipation space before the arena.

**Combat and height.** Prototype a 1,800–2,400-unit arena with an open readable centre, edge cover in two heights and a bank/platform elevation option. Marquee dressing can show wear and failed spectacle; structural collapse affecting collision is not implied as implemented.

**Secret.** A worn path behind a waterfall shelf or a riverbank gap rewards inspection. The greybox must prove its reachable legal surface and return route before obscuring it with water/foliage art.

**Art sub-palette.** Pine #445D4C, river #415D61, rock #77796D; board L25/L26/L27.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Canopies, water and theatre decoration must yield to boss tells. Bridge deck pivots, cliff faces and shore walkability need real depth/alignment checks. The new boss trigger/table belongs to W3, not an art toggle.
