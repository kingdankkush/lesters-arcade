# Hashwood River — area brief draft

Status: local River W2k geometry/nav/native-browser checkpoint passes; owner playtest, full area design and final art acceptance remain open. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** The river explains the bridges and settlement tracks. Forest routes alternate between enclosed banks and a broad clearing occupied by a travelling spectacle.

**Landmark.** A waterfall shelf and bridge silhouette; the Baron’s marquee is the secondary local landmark.

**Routes in and out.** City road, Bayou riverside trail, Rugpull Woods path and a darker Hollow Pines trail. Place crossings at narrow banks and provide more than one route across the area; do not force every run through the boss clearing.

**Objective staging.** Stage river equipment at a bridge or actual channel structure. The Rug Pull Baron entrance crosses a visible threshold into the marquee clearing, with anticipation space before the arena.

**Combat and height.** Prototype a 1,800–2,400-unit arena with an open readable centre, edge cover in two heights and a bank/platform elevation option. Marquee dressing can show wear and failed spectacle; structural collapse affecting collision is not implied as implemented.

**Secret.** A worn path behind a waterfall shelf or a riverbank gap rewards inspection. The greybox must prove its reachable legal surface and return route before obscuring it with water/foliage art.

**Art sub-palette.** Pine #445D4C, river #415D61, rock #77796D; board L25/L26/L27.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Canopies, water and theatre decoration must yield to boss tells. Bridge deck pivots, cliff faces and shore walkability need real depth/alignment checks. The new boss trigger/table belongs to W3, not an art toggle.

## W2k authored local layout

The four existing roads now meet a river landscape with a clear hierarchy:
City arrives on the upper bank, Woods joins farther east, and Bayou/Pines
follow the quiet lower-bank trail. A shaped channel separates the banks;
two north/south bridges cross its narrow section with existing 24-unit ramps.
The upper-bank route connects their approaches. Closing the entire southwest
Baron clearing leaves the regional trails and both crossings usable.

The capstan house belongs beside the City bridge, with its service objective
outside the southern face and a clear apron. The marquee backs the southern
clearing; a pair of posts gives its entrance a threshold without dividing the
open centre. Short and tall cover stay near the perimeter. The northwest shelf
puts the secret behind a physical cliff shape, with an upper return leg and
separate river-facing view. The east bank stays sparse so it reads as a trail,
not another courtyard full of repeated props.

Future art should follow these uses: compacted traffic on approaches, worn
boards across the decks, exposed stone around the waterfall shelf, damp bank
edges, willow/root clusters away from crossing silhouettes, and worn spectacle
materials around the marquee. Waterfall spray and canopy layers must leave
deck edges, shore walkability and boss tells clear. These are placement
intentions, not completed assets or approval to change collision.

Seven authored local inspection paths and sixteen pieces replace the generic
template. Gross water plus solid area is 1,670,700 units², below the old
2,060,800; the whole-world conservative grid remains 47.3637% walkable. The
1,800 × 1,800 court has two usable exits, both cover heights and 96.4444% open
samples. No Baron trigger, waterfall physics, collapse, swimming, climb/drop
control or official map version is activated.

Owner playtest, route pacing, whole-map approval, final area art and actual
phone performance remain required. The inherited River/Woods road is still
38.17 seconds at nominal 240 units/second, outside the brief's 10–25 target;
this slice preserves that authored road and records the warning openly.
