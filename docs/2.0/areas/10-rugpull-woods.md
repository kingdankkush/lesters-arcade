# Rugpull Woods — area brief draft

Status: preparatory layout intent; no geometry/nav/playtest/art acceptance yet. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** Several connected camps use nearby water, cover and lookout positions. Their tents, tracks and palisades reveal purpose and create a rhythm of occupied clearings and calm travel.

**Landmark.** A visible lookout above a palisade clearing, with distinct tent groupings for the camps.

**Routes in and out.** Trails to MWEB Meadows, Halving Farms and Hashwood River, plus the proposed second route to Hollow Pines. Camps sit beside the through paths rather than blocking all connections.

**Objective staging.** Each camp-clear objective belongs to a distinct camp space with approach, lookout and return route. Camps must not be indistinguishable clusters of tents. The exact objective/enemy table is deferred to the versioned map/gameplay slice.

**Combat and height.** Alternate two-exit camp clearings and calmer patrol roads. Palisade/building walls and lower supply stacks provide cover heights; an accessible bank/lookout route provides elevation. Patrol/ambush AI and balance remain separate tested gameplay work.

**Secret.** A side track beyond a camp boundary reveals an abandoned supply nook, then returns to the travel route. Use material wear/gaps as clues; do not let undergrowth hide invisible blockers.

**Art sub-palette.** Moss #56634E, wood #635948, canvas #96917E; board L30; camp-specific reference study still pending.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Measure distinct entrances, combat/quiet density and readable exits. Camp art waits for bible/target and playable-greybox approval. Watchtower traversal/cover and objective state must not activate through a presentation flag.
