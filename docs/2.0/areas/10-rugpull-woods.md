# Rugpull Woods — area brief draft

Status: local Woods W2i geometry/nav/native-browser checkpoint passes; owner playtest, full area design and art acceptance remain open. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** Several connected camps use nearby water, cover and lookout positions. Their tents, tracks and palisades reveal purpose and create a rhythm of occupied clearings and calm travel.

**Landmark.** A visible lookout above a palisade clearing, with distinct tent groupings for the camps.

**Routes in and out.** Trails to MWEB Meadows, Halving Farms and Hashwood River, plus the proposed second route to Hollow Pines. Camps sit beside the through paths rather than blocking all connections.

**Objective staging.** Each camp-clear objective belongs to a distinct camp space with approach, lookout and return route. Camps must not be indistinguishable clusters of tents. The exact objective/enemy table is deferred to the versioned map/gameplay slice.

**Combat and height.** Alternate two-exit camp clearings and calmer patrol roads. Palisade/building walls and lower supply stacks provide cover heights; an accessible bank/lookout route provides elevation. Patrol/ambush AI and balance remain separate tested gameplay work.

**Secret.** A side track beyond a camp boundary reveals an abandoned supply nook, then returns to the travel route. Use material wear/gaps as clues; do not let undergrowth hide invisible blockers.

**Art sub-palette.** Moss #56634E, wood #635948, canvas #96917E; board L30; camp-specific reference study still pending.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Measure distinct entrances, combat/quiet density and readable exits. Camp art waits for bible/target and playable-greybox approval. Watchtower traversal/cover and objective state must not activate through a presentation flag.

## W2i local layout checkpoint

Four unchanged regional approaches converge on a quiet western trail. The
occupied eastern camp sits beside this junction; closing its complete court
must leave the regional trail open. A northwestern root bank frames the River
arrival without hiding it, and the western woodland edge bends around the
abandoned southern stores rather than becoming another square corner mass.

Three places have different purposes. The eastern camp has a supply shelter,
palisade boundary and low stack with a clear objective frontage. The northern
lookout stands behind a shallow bank reached by ramps at both ends. The southern
abandoned store and lean-to face a returning side track; the secret inspection
point is visibly outside the store rather than inside a sealed prop. The
24-unit lookout remains the existing ramp contract, not a new watchtower climb.

Future terrain and prop placement should reinforce those purposes:

- Pack the through-trail where traffic converges and leave its shoulders open.
  Root clusters follow the outer bank, with isolated trunks framing approaches
  instead of evenly spaced trees across the route.
- Give the occupied supply frontage canvas, tied bundles and worn unloading
  ground. Keep the court centre and exits quiet for actor and warning clarity.
- Place lookout supports on firm bank ground, with local erosion at both ramp
  mouths. Avoid dense foliage where the two approaches need to remain visible.
- Let the abandoned camp's wear, sagging canvas and encroaching vegetation differ
  from active supplies. Use a worn gap to signal the returning stores trail.
- Use the southeast bank to transition from open camp ground to denser woodland,
  with plants tied to slope, shade and drainage rather than uniform filler.

Local source and browser checks pass; placeholder art remains. Camps do not yet clear, spawn
