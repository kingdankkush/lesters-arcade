# MWEB Meadows — area brief draft

Status: local Meadows W2b geometry/nav/native-browser checkpoint passes; owner playtest, full area design and final art acceptance remain open. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** The welcoming edge of a suburb where a maintained road gives way to neglected gardens. The first route should look safe and understandable before enemies establish the threat.

**Landmark.** A broad old oak beside a modest relay green; low houses frame it without hiding the route.

**Routes in and out.** Paved road to Litecoin City; farm access road to Halving Farms; dirt trail to Rugpull Woods; a long winding optional fortress service approach. Keep the city/farm junction visible from the entry green. The fortress route must advertise increasing danger and cannot be called progression-safe until shortest paths, spawn threats and owner playtest are measured; this brief invents no new lock or reward rule.

**Objective staging.** Stage the initial relay as civic equipment beside an access path. Put its switch on the player-facing side and leave room to approach from either flank. Keep tutorial prompts at distinct places along the route rather than stacking them at spawn.

**Combat and height.** An open garden green, then a narrower fence lane leading into a street court. Use a porch and low retaining bank for the first height option; low fences and a solid building wall illustrate the two cover types in the later versioned prototype. Each court has two readable exits.

**Secret.** A broken fence reveals a garden loop that rejoins the main lane. The clue is a change in worn ground and visible gap; no invisible trigger wall or mandatory pixel hunt.

**Art sub-palette.** Sage #78836B, earth #786750, chalk #D4D0BE; board L01/L02.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Entry, road junction, relay approach and both exits remain readable at phone framing. The initial combat floor stays quieter than the surrounding gardens. Measure spawn safety and actual path lengths in W1/W2; do not assign new difficulty or rewards from this document.
