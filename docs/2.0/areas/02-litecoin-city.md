# Litecoin City — area brief draft

Status: preparatory layout intent; no geometry/nav/playtest/art acceptance yet. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** A civic and financial centre with purposeful streets, a river crossing and signs of interrupted daily life. Navigation comes from street structure and skyline, with ecosystem branding in believable commercial locations.

**Landmark.** The exchange skyline and an elevated Closing Bell gantry, visible over a civic plaza.

**Routes in and out.** Paved spine to Silver Coast and MWEB Meadows; road south to Hashwood River; quarry approach toward Ledger Ridge. Side streets connect back to the spine rather than becoming identical dead ends.

**Objective staging.** Move the Closing Bell into an exchange forecourt in the future map contract. Its approach passes a clear gate into a readable plaza. A single slot-to-brand data file drives signs; official logos need verified primary sources, otherwise use names. No ranking/follower claims are encoded here.

**Combat and height.** Prototype a 1,800–2,400-unit Liquidator plaza with two approach lanes, peripheral tall/short cover and a visible elevated route. Keep the centre open for tells and dodges. Rooftop access and descent remain gated traversal prototypes; avoid art implying an inaccessible climb.

**Secret.** A service alley leads to a marked maintenance access and returns to another street. Reveal its opening with wear and architecture, keeping it distinct from the boss entrance.

**Art sub-palette.** Slate #4E5E62, stone #91948C, silver #B8C1BD; board L04/L05/L06.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Street junctions, roof edges and the arena entrance must read before decorative signs. Keep flashing/ticker signs out of the combat hierarchy. W3 must bind relocated boss/objective travel to the new map version while preserving the old district tables.
