# Litecoin City — area brief draft

Status: local City W2d geometry/nav/native-browser checkpoint passes; owner playtest, full area design and art acceptance remain open. Part of the continuous 20,000 × 14,000 world. See [brief index](INDEX.md) for shared scales, authorisation and gates.

**Story and flow.** A civic and financial centre with purposeful streets, a river crossing and signs of interrupted daily life. Navigation comes from street structure and skyline, with ecosystem branding in believable commercial locations.

**Landmark.** The exchange skyline and an elevated Closing Bell gantry, visible over a civic plaza.

**Routes in and out.** Paved spine to Silver Coast and MWEB Meadows; road south to Hashwood River; quarry approach toward Ledger Ridge. Side streets connect back to the spine rather than becoming identical dead ends.

**Objective staging.** Move the Closing Bell into an exchange forecourt in the future map contract. Its approach passes a clear gate into a readable plaza. A single slot-to-brand data file drives signs; official logos need verified primary sources, otherwise use names. No ranking/follower claims are encoded here.

**Combat and height.** Prototype a 1,800–2,400-unit Liquidator plaza with two approach lanes, peripheral tall/short cover and a visible elevated route. Keep the centre open for tells and dodges. Rooftop access and descent remain gated traversal prototypes; avoid art implying an inaccessible climb.

**Secret.** A service alley leads to a marked maintenance access and returns to another street. Reveal its opening with wear and architecture, keeping it distinct from the boss entrance.

**Art sub-palette.** Slate #4E5E62, stone #91948C, silver #B8C1BD; board L04/L05/L06.

**Greybox acceptance focus.** Street junctions, roof edges and the arena entrance must read before decorative signs. Keep flashing/ticker signs out of the combat hierarchy. W3 must bind relocated boss/objective travel to the new map version while preserving the old district tables.

## W2d local layout checkpoint

This local navigation slice gives the area a four-way civic junction and a
separate southeast exchange forecourt. High Street connects Coast and Meadows;
River Street connects Ridge and River. Closing the entire Liquidator space must
leave both streets and the service loop passable. The four regional road endpoints
stay fixed. This is local preview geometry, not an official map or mission change.

The exchange's long north-facing frontage anchors Closing Bell. Two street
approaches reach different edges of the plaza. The centre is intentionally open;
short bench cover and a tall civic wall sit toward the perimeter. An east-side
gantry has an actual shallow ramp and deck. It does not grant a new climb, drop,
combat height advantage or interaction rule. The northwest market frontage and
lower warehouse form a narrower maintenance alley which rejoins the Ridge road.
Southwest housing and its small service shed have a different footprint and
height from the taller northern commercial block and broad exchange.

Future terrain and prop placement follows use, not even distribution:

- Public streets: broad, consistently readable paving; worn crossing corners,
  drains at the edges, and sparse obstacles outside movement and firing lines.
- Commercial frontage: larger facade rhythm and shop thresholds facing the
  street, with signs attached to plausible storefronts rather than free-floating
  landmarks. Verified brand artwork is a later asset task.
- Service lane: rougher patched surface, loading wear and utilities beside the
  warehouse walls. Crates or bins can cluster at recesses only if the tested
  walking corridor remains clear; the exit must be legible from inside the lane.
- Exchange plaza: cleaner stone and a strong approach axis toward the bell,
  restrained peripheral benches/planters, and a largely empty combat centre.
  Focal detail belongs at the exchange entrance, not across dodge space.
- River housing: smaller repeated domestic elements within a distinct block,
  softer edge wear and limited planted pockets. Utility access belongs beside
  the rear shed. Foliage must not imply a passage through solid buildings.
- Gantry: exposed structural edges and understandable supports, with the ramp
  entrance clearly visible. No decorative ladder should promise an unavailable
  control or traversal path.

City uses one displaced street label and short unique site labels. Global plan
label scale remains a separate preview readability issue. These are placement
intentions for later approved art, not produced textures or accepted final art.
The art bible/target slice, owner area playtest, official versioned map/verifier
review, combat pacing, full-width travel and physical phone checks remain open.
