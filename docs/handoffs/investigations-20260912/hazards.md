# Implementation brief: hazards (package order 3)

## Ranking rationale
The only simulation slice in the package and the largest gameplay win: four authored hazards that are currently pure decoration become real (rockfall and liquidation-grid periodic damage with telegraphs, spore-bed slow, conveyor drift affecting player and enemies). Deterministic by construction (pure functions of tick % period and position, no RNG, no per-run state). Scoped to the core hazards only; explosive zones are deferred because they collide with destructibles on the target-list regions and carry an owner-facing XP-attribution choice.
