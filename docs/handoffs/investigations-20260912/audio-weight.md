# Investigation: audio-weight

The 37 HMH cues are rendered by scripts/build-hmh-weapon-sfx.py from a four-kind layer model — 'noise' (local xorshift32, lines 25–35), 'tone' (sine), 'metal' (five inharmonic modes, line 238) and 'growl' (formant stack, 241–248) — where each layer (line 48–51) carries gain/decayMs/startMs/attackMs/hz/sweep (exponential pitch glide, line 231)/lowpassHz/highpassHz/lengthMs, every filter is single-pole (38–45, 257–259), and each cue then passes through tanh(drive) saturation (262), a double 9 kHz lowpass (265), 25 Hz DC rejection (268–269) and peak normalisation to min(peak, 0.78) (270–275); there is no dedicated sub layer — low end is just a 'tone' at 66–158 Hz inside the shot() helper (60–67). The manifest (build() 296–312) records src/bytes/sha256/runtimeCue/full synth recipe and a pcm block limited to peak, rms, dcMean, crestDb, clippedSamples (pcm_metrics 287–293); --verify-reproducible only re-renders in-process and compares bytes (301–302). No test pins literal hashes: tests/hmh-reboot-action-sfx.test.mjs:68–81 recomputes each WAV's sha256 against the manifest and enforces peak ≤ .781, |DC| < .008, silent boundary samples, < 1.6 s and ≤ 1,750,000 total bytes (currently 1,390,778), lines 27–35 require coin-blaster/auto-miner 15–75 ms body RMS ≥ .12 and weaponFireGain ≥ .18, and tests/hmh-reboot-weapon-audio.test.mjs:65–72 pins pipelineId 'hmh-weapon-sfx-v2'. npm audio:report (scripts/hmh-audio-system-report.mjs:32–48) measures nothing acoustic — it cross-checks registry vs manifest and prints mix caps; audio:audition (scripts/hmh-audio-audition.mjs:21–50) renders the registry fallback tones, not the WAVs; only the manually-run scripts/hmh-action-audio-audit.mjs (not in package.json) measures shipped PCM (peak, 4x-oversampled reconstructed peak, rms, routed rms, a 154-event dense mix with max voices, lines 69–137). Mix caps live in apps/portal/src/hmh-audio-system.mjs:111–116 (maxVoices 32, familyCaps weapon 16/impact 14/damage 4/boss 4, maxSynthVolume 0.16) and resolveHmhSfxCuePlan (132–166) clamps every routed volume to [0.008, 0.16], so scatter-shotgun, launcher-rig, hash-rail, grenade-boom and critical player-hit are already pinned at the ceiling while auto-miner routes at 0.105 and bear-market-burner at 0.087; the child clamps to 16 voices and multiplies bus/night/boss-duck in apps/hmh-reboot/src/combat-audio.mjs:71,156–162 and plays music at 0.4 × 0.7 (line 120). A read-only pure-Python probe I ran over the shipped WAVs shows why the guns lack Doom/Duke weight: the four core fire cues have crest factors of only 8.8–10.4 dB with the 0–8 ms transient within −1.8…+2.2 dB of the 15–75 ms body (the tanh drive of 1.7 on ~5 summed gain flattens the crack), spectral centroids of 229–309 Hz, and a binary low end — coin-blaster/auto-miner/forked/lightning carry ≈0 % energy below 100 Hz while scatter-shotgun/launcher-rig/hash-rail carry 83–92 % below 100 Hz with < 2 % in the 100–250 Hz punch band — plus −30 dB tails of 163 ms on the 47 ms-cooldown auto-miner and 506 ms on the launcher. Nothing spectral, transient/tail, category-loudness or per-family voice-count is measured anywhere in the repo today (grep for centroid/lufs/spectral/goertzel/fft finds only comments and gameplay words).

## Gaps
- No per-cue transient/body/tail measurement: pcm_metrics() (build-hmh-weapon-sfx.py:287-293) records only peak, rms, dcMean, crestDb, clippedSamples; attack time, 0–8 ms transient vs 15–75 ms body ratio, −30 dB tail length and late-energy fraction are absent
- No spectral measurement anywhere (centroid, sub-100 Hz fraction, 100–250 Hz punch, presence band): grep for centroid|lufs|spectral|goertzel|fft in scripts/, tests/, apps/hmh-reboot/src, apps/portal/src finds only comments and gameplay vocabulary
- No per-family loudness or simultaneous-voice-count measurement: hmh-action-audio-audit.mjs reports one dense-mix peak/rms and a single global maxVoices (lines 114-137); audio:report prints caps without measuring; audio:audition measures fallback tones, not WAVs
- No numeric targets by weapon family exist in any file; the only pinned acoustic thresholds are peak ≤ .781, body RMS ≥ .12 for two cues, rms > .02, DC < .008
- Transient is not 'clear': measured 0–8 ms RMS sits −1.8…+2.2 dB relative to the 15–75 ms body for coin-blaster/auto-miner/scatter-shotgun/launcher-rig because the crack layer is summed before tanh(drive 1.7) (render_cue lines 260-262); there is no post-saturation layer path
- Low end is binary: no dedicated sub layer kind; pistol-class cues have ≈0 % energy < 100 Hz while shotgun/launcher/hash-rail carry 83–92 % < 100 Hz with < 2 % in 100–250 Hz, so the heavy guns vanish on laptop speakers
- Tails are uncontrolled relative to cooldowns: auto-miner −30 dB tail 163 ms on a 47 ms cooldown, coin-blaster 223 ms on 42 ms, launcher 506 ms; the audit's 7-voice peak is mostly overlapping weapon tails
- The 0.16 maxSynthVolume ceiling (hmh-audio-system.mjs:115) already clips five cues (scatter-shotgun, launcher-rig, hash-rail, grenade-boom, critical player-hit) so registry gainMul and HMH_WEAPON_SFX gains cannot express a weight hierarchy above it; routed whole-file RMS of every weapon is −28…−36 dBFS while music plays at 0.28 linear

- hmh-action-audio-audit.mjs is not reachable from any npm script (only listed in syntax-check.mjs:44) and writes to .tmp, so its numbers are not part of verify:full
- Reproducibility is only checked in-process (build script lines 301-302); the manifest does not record the interpreter/platform although the checkpoint pinned Python 3.12.14 and the local machine now runs 3.14.5

## Proposed change
All of this is offline tooling plus projection-only runtime constants; nothing touches simulation.

1. New pure-Python analyser: scripts/hmh_sfx_metrics.py (stdlib only — math, cmath, struct, json, argparse; runs on the Vercel CPython 3.12 + Pillow image and on 3.14 locally).
   - def analyse(samples: list[float], sample_rate: int) -> dict returning, rounded to 3–4 dp so manifest diffs stay readable: peak, rms, crestDb, peakMs, attack90Ms (first 1 ms RMS window ≥ 90 % of envelope peak), tail30dbMs (ms from envelope peak until every later 1 ms window stays below peak −30 dB), transientDb (RMS 0–8 ms), body15to75Db, transientMinusBodyDb, lateEnergyFrac (energy after 120 ms / total), centroidHz, and bands {sub20_100, punch100_250, lowMid250_1k, presence1k_4k, air4k} as energy fractions. Spectrum = 4096-point Hann frames, hop 2048, iterative radix-2 FFT, energy-summed across frames (10.8 Hz resolution; ≈0.2 s per cue in pure Python, ≈10 s for 37 cues — acceptable for an offline build). My scratchpad prototype of exactly this produced the baseline numbers quoted in the summary.
   - def check(manifest: dict, targets: dict) -> list[str] (human-readable violations) and a CLI: --report writes docs/audio/hmh-sfx-weight-metrics.json and .md (table per cue + family targets + pass/fail); --check exits 1 on any violation; --self-test renders a 440 Hz sine (centroid within 1 %), a 60 Hz sine (sub20_100 ≥ .95), and a click + 300 ms noise tail (transientMinusBodyDb ≥ +10, tail30dbMs within ±20 ms) and exits 0/1.
