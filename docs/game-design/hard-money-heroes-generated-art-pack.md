# Hard Money Heroes — Generated Art Pack

Generated for Lester's Arcade / Hard Money Heroes as local prototype concept art. These assets are **not final production artwork** and should be treated as direction, placeholder art, extraction source material, and portal polish until a human cleanup/slicing pass is complete.

## Safety / brand notes

- Prompts deliberately avoided official Litecoin marks, the `Ł` symbol, and heavy logo usage.
- Generated models may still invent accidental text, pseudo-logos, or letter-like marks. Review before production use.
- Prefer `*-textfree.png` versions in the portal.
- Final commercial/pay-to-play use still needs brand/legal review.
