## WO-81 Animation principles QA gates

| Gate | Required on | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| anticipation | hero attacks, enemy attacks, boss slams | 2 to 5 readable frames before contact, with silhouette or VFX pullback |
| smear | fast melee, dash, projectile launch | one directional smear or stretched accent frame during the fastest motion |
| impact | enemy hit, boss impact, player hurt | hit flash plus body offset plus particles or ground spark in the contact frame |
| follow-through | weapon swings, cape/coat/steam props | secondary motion resolves after contact before returning to idle |
| loop-bob | idle loops, hover enemies, ambient props | subtle two-axis bob or breath cycle that does not affect hitbox scale |
