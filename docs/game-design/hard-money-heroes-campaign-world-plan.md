# Hard Money Heroes Campaign World Plan

## Scope of this pass
- Lock Level 1 into an authored **Crypto Wasteland** route instead of a random prop soup.
- Stage Level 2 as **Litecoin City** with stronger urban/luxury neighborhood contrast.
- Treat roads, pathways, grass, water, trees, flowers, fences, gates, and interactive blockers as a connected level grammar.
- Use the existing runtime's district grid + road network as the macro backbone, then place setpieces, props, and interactive anchors with intent.

## Level 1 - Crypto Wasteland

### Moment-to-moment timeline
1. **0:00-5:00 — Survive the wasteland loop**
   - Player learns the main road spine and the safe side paths.
   - Smaller authored landmarks repeat: gas stop ruins, ghost-town lots, culvert crossings, yard fences, wrecked checkpoint gates.
2. **5:00 — Rug Pull Baron boss pressure**
   - A scripted boss enters the authored district loop.
   - Roads and open lots become the arena so the player can kite, rotate, and use sightlines.
3. **8:00 — Extraction beacon goes live**
   - A safehouse beacon spawns with guidance to the final authored objective.
   - Player must travel the route alive to clear Level 1.
4. **Post-clear — Continue button**
   - Show a highlight card with score, time, kills, and extraction grade.
   - Continue transitions into Level 2 loading: **Litecoin City**.

### District order and asset intent

#### 1. Desert Approach
- **Ground**: sand, gravel, scrub patches, dry creek edges.
- **Road system**: cracked two-lane asphalt with shoulder rubble.
- **Vegetation**: sparse grass, cactus/scrub, a few wind-bent trees.
- **Interactive blockers**: barricades, tire walls, culvert edges, checkpoint rails.
- **Purpose**: onboarding space and visual reset from the menu/loading screen.

#### 2. Ghost Town
- **Ground**: dust, hardpack, plank walks, abandoned concrete pads.
- **Road/path**: main road with side alleys between storefront shells.
- **Vegetation**: dead planters, dried weeds, isolated flower beds gone wild.
- **Interactive setpieces**: boarded saloon, loot kiosk shell, fenced back lot.
- **Purpose**: the first real authored cluster; teaches how props form lanes.

#### 3. Country Road
- **Ground**: asphalt spine, gravel pull-offs, ditch banks, dirt side paths.
- **Water**: drainage channels and a shallow runoff crossing.
- **Vegetation**: roadside trees, shoulder grass, wildflower strips.
- **Interactive blockers**: road signs, culvert pipes, wrecked trucks, service barriers.
- **Purpose**: create long sightlines and a cleaner kiting lane before pressure spikes.

#### 4. Residential Edge
- **Ground**: patchy lawns, driveways, sidewalks, yard dirt.
- **Road/path**: suburban loops feeding back into the main road.
- **Water**: retention pond / storm runoff pockets.
- **Vegetation**: trees, shrubs, flowers, backyard grass clusters.
- **Interactive blockers**: fences, gates, sheds, mailboxes, cul-de-sac medians.
- **Purpose**: strongest authored density before the extraction zone.

#### 5. Inner City Threshold / Safehouse Belt
- **Ground**: cracked city asphalt, concrete lots, medians, abandoned fountain edges.
- **Road/path**: intersection hub with one clear extraction route.
- **Vegetation**: tougher urban shrubs, median grass, fountain weeds.
- **Interactive blockers**: jersey barriers, security gates, checkpoint booths.
- **Purpose**: make the extraction feel earned and spatially legible.

### Level 1 asset grammar
- **Primary route**: asphalt road spine always readable at a glance.
- **Secondary route**: dirt path, sidewalk, alley, or yard shortcut that reconnects to the spine.
- **Soft boundary dressing**: grass, flowers, rocks, and shrubs hug edges and landmarks rather than filling every empty tile.
- **Hard blockers**: fences, buildings, walls, water, culverts, barriers, and wrecks define combat pockets and line-of-sight breaks.
- **Water use**: only as authored channels, ponds, and runoff separators; not random full-biome patches.
- **Tree use**: at road bends, lot edges, yards, and drainage banks where they reinforce the route silhouette.

### Needed Level 1 asset additions / polish
- Road corners, T-junctions, lane breaks, and shoulder caps.
- Culvert entrances, ditch transitions, and drainage bridges.
- Fence kits with gates and broken variants.
- Extraction beacon / safehouse signage.
- Roadside checkpoint props, warning signs, and medians.
- Interactive blockers with cleaner hit detection footprints.
- More coherent tileset groupings for sand ↔ gravel ↔ asphalt ↔ lawn transitions.

## Level 2 - Litecoin City

### World pitch
This level should feel like the player pushed from the wasteland into the real machine: denser streets, richer lighting, more vertical framing, and clearer contrast between the public inner city and wealthy private neighborhoods.

### District order
1. **Outer Boulevard** — alleys, tram lanes, corner stores, service roads.
2. **Financial Core** — tower lobbies, plazas, ticker screens, security barriers.
3. **Luxury Neighborhoods** — gated homes, pools, hedges, sculpture gardens, chauffeur loops.
4. **Penthouse Rim** — rooftop gardens, helipads, skybridges, VIP extraction lanes.

### Level 2 asset grammar
- Use **boulevards + alleys** as the primary route pair.
- Use **sidewalk medians, planters, fountains, pools, and hedge walls** as authored combat separators.
- Urban greenery should be intentional: medians, courtyards, rooftop gardens, luxury yards.
- Interactive props should skew toward **traffic lights, elevators, gates, parking booms, security desks, luxury barriers, skybridge chokepoints**.

### Level 2 enemy roster seeds
- Bitcoin Maximalist Riot Cops
- NFT Valets
- Stablecoin Socialites
- DAO Lobbyists
- Influencer Camera Drones
- Chainlink Security Clerks
- Boss: **Mr. NGMI** in the penthouse / rooftop district
