## Deliberate HALTs and residual risk

1. **No contract broadcast in this release.** Portal deployment is approved; contract deployment remains separately gated by the exact manifest and an explicit transaction-level approval.
2. **Verifier operations are not production-ready.** The dry-run config currently points the verifier role at the deployer for deterministic preparation. Replace it with an independently controlled verifier service/signer and review its key custody before broadcast.
3. **Paid settlement remains disabled.** PaymentRouter, ArcadePaymentRouter, SessionLedger, AchievementRegistry, TournamentPool, and LestersArcadeCore are excluded.
4. **Physical-device evidence is automated/emulated on this host.** Touch/controller source and viewport gates pass, but a named physical controller and real touch device were not available for a human acceptance pass.
5. **Sustained browser evidence is bounded.** Active real-browser combat and performance sampling passed, while 45-minute balance evidence is deterministic simulation, not a fabricated claim of a 45-minute human session.
