no actions, so the procedural poser drives every clip either way.

## 3. What the exporter contract had to learn

- **Camera.** The package (4.1, 5.10, section 6) puts new actors on the hero
  camera at 35 degrees below the horizon with `hmh-light-rig.json`. The shipped
  heroes and the core six still render at 55 degrees (their manifests say
  `cameraPitchDegrees: 55`; `create-hmh-production-hero-pilot.py` derives the
  camera height from it), so until the S4.8 re-render lands the Liquidator is
  the only 35 degree actor in the world. That is the package's own sequence,
  recorded here so nobody reads the mixed cameras as a defect of this pilot.
- **Light rig.** `lightRigFamily: hero` uses the shared colours with the hero
  energies (560 / 340 / 480). Under that key a lit `#ff496c` base clips to
  pink-white in AgX. The accent is therefore emission-led: base dimmed to 0.18
  of the accent, emission strength 0.7 (2.0 still desaturated), and the
  exporter's per-phase recolour multiplies the base by `hmh_accent_base_scale`
  so the dimming survives the phase swap. The ring reads crimson-salmon; the
  constants are in one place in the derivative builder if the owner wants it
  deeper.
- **Frame size and ortho.** 192 px frames as the shipped boss. The ortho is
  the size knob (the exporter normalises every derivative to 1.75 m and the
  boss's build lives in `cameraOrthoScale`, exactly as the procedural roster
  manifest does). Measured, not tuned by feel: see section 4.
- **Textures.** The roster path renders the Tripo PBR set (colour, normal,
  ORM) straight under the shared rig like the shipped five; the external
  pipeline's `HMH_LookDev_v1` band shader and waist split are hero-pipeline
  features (layered atlases) and were not applied because the roster renders a
  single body layer. The pipeline doc's C-1 limitation (packed textures counted
  as external dependencies) lives in the hero runner and did not bite here.

## 4. The size cap, measured

The design asks for about 2.4 m, 90-95 world px (about 1.8x an ordinary
enemy). The current single-page contract cannot hold that:

| Ortho | Idle body height (192 px frame) | World px (x0.86) | 840 padded frames | Lossless WebP (measured ~1.0 byte per sprite pixel) | Fits one 2048 page? |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 3.5 | 89 px | 77 | 5.08 M px | ~4.8 MB | no (page 4.19 M px, cap 4 MiB) |
| 3.8 | 82 px | 70 (today's shipped boss) | 4.33 M px | ~4.1 MB | no |
| 4.1 | 76 px | 65 | 3.75 M px | ~3.75 MB | edge to edge |
| **4.15** | **76 px (packed)** | **65** | 3.60 M px sprite area, page fill 0.908 | **3,007,902 bytes** (0.835 byte per sprite pixel) | **yes, 2048 x 1936** |

The shipped Bagholder-body boss packed 840 frames at 89 percent of the page;
the human body at 35 degrees is taller per frame and wider at the tell, so at
the same ortho it no longer fits. The pilot therefore ships him about 9
percent under today's world size (64 versus 70.5 world px; 1.26x the shipped
Bagholder's 50.5). The design size needs the boss-schema extension the package
assigns to the Baron slice (4.1: about 1,100-1,200 frames, reduced re-baked
phase 2/3 subsets, one resident boss atlas with an unload path, a 128 px
mobile tier). Reduced phase 2/3 subsets alone were measured too: 584 frames
fit at ortho 3.4-3.5 (about 77 world px) but break the 840-frame pins in
`hmh-native-roster-adoption.test.mjs`, `hmh-reboot-enemy-roster-atlas.test.mjs`
and `hmh-reboot-production-asset-qa.mjs` plus the fixed clip counts in four
pipeline scripts, so they belong with that extension, not with a projection
swap.

Roster wire budget after the swap: 14,892,353 bytes (14.2 MiB) of the 16 MiB
`maxRosterAtlasTotalBytes`, down from 15,155,687 with the shipped boss
(`npm run assets:qa:hmh-reboot`: pass). Decoded page 2048 x 1936 x 4 =
15,859,712 bytes (15.1 MiB), against 16,646,144 before; native decoded total
66,322,432 bytes.

## 6. Deviations from the step brief, stated

- **Clips.** Six states per phase (idle, run, tell, attack, hit, death), the
  runtime's pinned set. The 4.3 boss clips (dash, the three tell variants,
  halt, kneel, intro, transitions) need the roster-schema extension before an
  atlas can carry them; authoring them now would render frames no code can
  select.
- **Phase dressing.** Phase 2 adds gold margin spikes on the epaulettes, phase
  3 adds the violet halo; both recolour the crown band. "Coat off" and "tie
  torn, crown cracked" are mesh edits on a single Tripo texture set and were
  not attempted as accessory swaps.
- **Gavel.** Authored Blender primitives on `weapon_socket` (steel head, brass
  caps, wrapped handle, head-up in the right fist), not a 40-credit prop
  generation: the casting doc keeps simple mechanical props as primitives and
  nothing of a textured hammer survives a 20 px head.
- **Mobile tier.** No 128 px tier was rendered; the runtime has no tier
  selection and the boss is not a new enemy under the 5.10 gate. The decoded
  page is measured above.
- **Blind read.** The casting doc's two-session blind read of the head crop
  is still owed; this workflow is non-interactive.
