# Parent Ranked contract-preflight acceptance

Scope: the source-guard portion of `WEB3:B-2`, not full Web3 or website-release completion.

## Required behavior

- The parent binds preflight to the actual selected canonical game ID and LitVM LiteForge chain 4441. Wrong-chain reads stop before contract, account and funding inspection.
- At one pinned block, require bytecode at the configured GameRegistry and score registry, the selected game record and identity, a nonzero confirmed developer wallet, playable approval, matching score-to-game registry wiring, a nonzero configured verifier, and a decodable current eleven-field score tuple.
- No legacy decode fallback may grant verification, ranking, achievement or settlement authority. A compatibility probe is not full deployed-bytecode verification or verifier-service acceptance.
- Preflight requests existing accounts only. It never requests account access, creates a signer, signs or broadcasts. Invalid explicit gas estimates fail closed.
- The modal must consume the complete result, not just chain/funds booleans. Missing UI, contract errors, cancellation, stale read completion and selected-cabinet changes must not admit a live session.
- The direct Ranked writer respects `SETTLEMENT_LIVE=false`; a source-isolated live-path test must prove contract approval is rechecked before signer access. Tests do not toggle the actual flag.
- Preserve Free Mode, network-free local Ranked preview, existing profile/provenance/achievement work, Chikun boundaries, the STACKED pause, the mixed index, generated bundles and Codex's child-world/control lane.
