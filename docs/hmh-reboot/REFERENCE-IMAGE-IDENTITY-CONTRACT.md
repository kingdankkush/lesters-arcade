Status: source-observation contract; **no replacement model has passed likeness or runtime acceptance**.
Scope: the eight owner-supplied PNGs in `apps/hmh-reboot/assets/source/reference/heroes/`.
Provenance authority: `apps/hmh-reboot/assets/source/reference/heroes/references.json`.

This replaces the delegated draft, whose anatomical-side, costume, expression and provenance claims were not reliable. The observations below were checked directly against the full images and native-resolution crops. The source PNGs win over any written transcription, including this document. Do not copy the rejected draft's hash table or its claim that 2D references establish Blender/world axes.

## Reading rules

- `front.png` is a single full-body illustration. `turnaround.png` is a front/side/back sheet, with expression crops for Lester and Lilly only.
- Viewer-left/right always refers to the specified image panel. In a front-facing panel, viewer-left corresponds to anatomical right. A back view reverses that relationship.
- Both supplied images guide each character. Use the single illustration for close likeness/material detail and the sheet for volume and rear construction; do not silently mirror accessories to fit an earlier handoff.
- Preserve genuine illustration differences as review items. Small badges, scars and partially obscured accessories need native crops before transcription.
- Image appearance cannot establish topology, rig quality, exact model height, animation quality, ownership terms for a generated export, or game performance.

## Lit Commando (`lit-commando`)

Direct front-image and sheet-front observations:

- Muscular adult human man, square jaw, stern brows, visible stubble; dark shaggy mullet with a swept fringe and hair extending behind the neck.
- Red neckerchief at the throat, not a red forehead headband.
- Olive open field shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbows over a dark tank top; layered tactical harness and pouches. This is not a sleeveless outfit.
- Large raised shoulder armor is prominent on **viewer-right / anatomical left**. A circular unit patch is visible on **viewer-left / anatomical right** upper sleeve. Preserve the detailed shoulder webbing rather than reducing the shoulders to a symmetric pair of blocks.
- Red cloth is wrapped around **viewer-right / anatomical left** arm near the rolled sleeve/elbow. Do not call it red wristbands on both arms.
- Red-and-brass shells descend from **viewer-right shoulder to viewer-left waist**, i.e. anatomical left shoulder toward right hip.
- Rectangular metal belt buckle, olive pouches, hanging carabiner, dark hip holster on viewer-right, thigh straps and camouflage cargo trousers.
- Riveted dark knee pads, fingerless armored gloves, tall black lace-up boots with brass-toned hardware.
- The sheet contains three full-body panels and no separate expression row.

Source review must retain the neckerchief, shell direction, visible face, rolled sleeves and asymmetry at gameplay scale. Do not promote the existing procedural experiment merely because its structure passes.

## Lit Valkyrie (`lit-valkyrie`)

Direct front-image and sheet-front observations:

- Athletic adult human woman with long, voluminous, wavy blonde hair; the hair extends far below the shoulders. It is not a short bob or the old mandatory platinum braid/ponytail design.
- Red forehead headband with trailing ends; necklace/dog tag; olive cropped tank exposing midriff and layered waist/chest webbing.
- Prominent worn metal pauldron on **viewer-right / anatomical left shoulder**.
- Red cloth armband is also on **viewer-right / anatomical left upper arm**, beneath the pauldron, in both supplied front presentations. Earlier claims placing it on anatomical right are rejected.
- Brass ammunition descends from **viewer-left shoulder to viewer-right waist**, i.e. anatomical right shoulder toward left hip. This is opposite Commando's shell direction.
- Camouflage cargo trousers, asymmetrical thigh pouches/straps, riveted knee protection, fingerless gloves and rugged black lace-up boots.
- Back presentation shows long loose/wavy hair with a gathered upper section and headband tie. Do not invent a cape or quiver.
- The sheet has three full-body views and no separate expression row.

## Lester (`lester-original`)

- Large glossy cobalt-blue spherical mascot head on an athletic human body; not a human helmet.
- White slashed Litecoin `Ł` spans the face, with large blue eyes, dark brows and a broad friendly mouth. In the single illustration both eyes are open. Do not turn the logo into a small forehead badge or require a permanent wink.
- The logo's placement/scale varies between the single illustration and sheet presentations. Preserve the recognizable face-spanning mark and compare the finished front render against the actual single illustration rather than certifying a coordinate from prose.
- Blue neckerchief with rear tails and blue wrist wraps.
- Dark short-sleeved shirt beneath olive tactical vest/webbing. The single illustration does not show royal-blue shirt sleeves.
- In the single front illustration, shoulder plate is on **viewer-left / anatomical right**; brass bandolier descends from **viewer-right shoulder to viewer-left waist**. Do not infer a shoulder from a rear panel using front-view conventions.
- Utility belt, rectangular bright buckle, pouches, tan/camouflage trousers, thigh straps, knee protection, fingerless gloves and black combat boots.
- Sheet: three full-body views plus **six** facial expression crops, covering happy/grinning, angry/shouting, surprised and sly/smug variants.
- Keep editable facial source and expression textures/actions. A rigid sphere head can retain expression swaps while the body is genuinely skinned.

## Lilly (`lilly`)

- Adult human woman, long layered wavy teal hair, visible face and round thin-framed glasses with teal-tinted lenses.
- Hair falls down the back toward the waist; it is the coat, not hair, that extends toward the calves. The single illustration includes a narrow face-framing braided strand. Do not replace the hair with an invented high ponytail or a few rectangular blocks.
- Long open teal/dark leather coat with gold angular/circuit-line trim, worn/distressed hem, visible lapels/collar and sleeve emblem. The single illustration's emblem is prominent on viewer-right sleeve. Check sleeve-side consistency across panels during source authoring rather than asserting an unobserved global coordinate.
- Dark fitted/laced corset-like top and a broad belt with an angular gold-rimmed buckle carrying a light `L`. The single illustration's buckle has clipped/octagonal corners, not a plain square gold plaque.
- Teal trousers with gold accents, thigh straps/pouches, riveted dark knee pads, fingerless gauntlets and black boots with gold/brass-toned details.
- Preserve the **long split coat tails** as an identity feature and bake secondary motion. The older instruction to remove the long coat is superseded by these supplied images.
- Sheet: three full-body views plus **five** expression crops. Do not import expression heads as additional full-body subjects into image-to-3D generation.
