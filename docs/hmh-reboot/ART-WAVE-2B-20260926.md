## 2. Why a separate boss path

The shipped roster contract (`enemy-roster-atlas.mjs`) demands six states
x eight directions x every phase on one 2048 page, which already caps the
Liquidator below his design size (ART-WAVE-1 section 4). A boss needs what
LEVEL-1-DESIGN-PACKAGE 4.1 lists: a full phase-1 clip set, reduced re-baked
subsets for phases 2 and 3, south-only intro, transition and death clips,
256 px desktop source, 128 px mobile, and one resident boss atlas. Rather
than change the pinned 840-frame contract that wave 1 and the shipped boss
depend on, the district bosses get a sibling pipeline id
(`hmh-reboot-boss-roster-v1`) with its own manifest, metadata schema and
binder. Wave 1's Rug Pull Baron can adopt the same path if it wants to.

A clip missing from a later phase falls back to the nearest earlier phase

**Phase dressings** (accessory swaps on the one rig, `hmh_visible_phases`):

- Lockkeeper: phase 2 soaks him to the chest (the exporter darkens and glosses
  the body below 60 percent of the chest bone) and brightens the six padlock
  lamps; phase 3 adds a snapped chain coiled round the key shaft and blazes
  the lamps (emission 0.9 / 1.2 / 2.0).
- Foreman: phase 2 lights the gauge and leaks steam from the valve; phase 3
  ruptures the tank top (peeled brass petals over a soot hole), adds a grey
  plume and a crack across the hat, and turns the headlamp lens `#ff476f`
  (casting 2.7: B's red lamp and ruptured tank as the phase-3 reference).

**Props.** The Lockkeeper's windlass key is a Blender primitive build (casting
2.8): an iron shaft as tall as he is, a square socket foot, the L crank and a
brass handle at the top, built upright in his idle grip on `weapon_socket` so
it reads as the L of "a barrel with an L" from the hero camera. The padlock
lamps and the Foreman's headlamp are located from each model's own texture
(hue, saturation, value, a grid clustering), not guessed.

## 4. Deviations, stated

- **Foreman input.** Casting 2.7 keeps A and names a prompt-a2 A-pose re-run
  as the fallback if the hunched pose fails the rig check. A's right arm is
  bent forward across the body (the retargeter assumes an A-pose rest) and it
  carries a goatee, so the re-run was taken before any 40-credit spend: the
  first (5 credits) came back with a jackhammer and a goatee and was rejected;
  the second passed every 4.5 item. B remains the phase-3 dressing reference.
- **Lockkeeper fixes.** Taken at input time rather than model time: lamps
  retinted in the concept (so the texture already carries `#ff476f`) and the
  shadow matted. The coat already hangs open over the cream sweater in A.
  Legs shortened to 0.9 at the source stage (casting "about 10 percent").
  The edge-contrast gate on the Crossing's water (casting 2.8 condition) needs
  the Crossing floor, which is layout v2's; it is owed to the browser review.
- **Tripo rig.** The Lockkeeper's pre-check said "not riggable"; a forced
  biped rig on the same model id (25 credits) rigged it cleanly.
- **Hit flinch.** One `hit` clip in eight directions; the package's separate
  front and back flinch would add 24 frames per phase and the eight
  directions already give the back view.
- **Clip trims.** Walk 8 frames and a 6-frame charge loop keep both bosses
  inside the 1,000-1,200 frame band the package budgets.
- **Leg squash** is a piecewise vertical map (below the hip line scaled,
  above shifted), applied to vertices and bone ends, then the model is
  rescaled to 2.3 m.
- **Blind read / originality.** Not applicable: neither boss's face is on the
  casting board's flag list; both read as their concepts.

## 6. Recipe for the next boss

1. Concept through casting; `scripts/hmh-tripo-input-hygiene.py` (matte,
   retint) and read the result.
2. `tripo make <input> --for game-pc --then rig-check,rig -o <vault dir>`; if
   the pre-check refuses a clean biped, `tripo anim rig <model task>
   --rig-type biped --spec mixamo`.
3. Add the boss to `hmh-boss-roster.json` and its clips to
   `hmh_boss_clips.py`; add an accessory builder to
   `build-hmh-boss-derivative.py`.
4. `--stage source` (design height, `--leg-scale` if the casting says so),
   commit through LFS; `--stage derivative`; `export-hmh-boss-roster.py
   --preview`; read it.
5. `build-hmh-boss-roster.py render --run a`, `--run b` (Blender slot),
   `pack`, read both contact sheets, `adopt`, `node --test
   tests/hmh-boss-roster-atlas.test.mjs`.
